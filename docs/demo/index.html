What you are seeing

  • Skinned meshes deformed on the GPU, joint matrices in a float texture (67 and 99 bones).
  • Three model formats: binary FBX (Ely), ASCII FBX (Vampire) and COLLADA (the Vampire's crouch clip).
  • Animator: crossfades, speed, loop, in-place root motion; clips shared across files and formats.
  • Shadows from the sun, point and spot lights in one shadow atlas.
  • Linear HDR lighting with tone mapping, saturation and contrast.
  • Render to texture: the monitor shows a second camera live.
Characters and animations from Mixamo. Built with the Omega engine.
